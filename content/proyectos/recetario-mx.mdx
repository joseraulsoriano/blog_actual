> Full platform: search, maps, auth, and MCP

### Destacados

- **Core features** — Search, filters, step-by-step recipes, images, authentication.
- **MCP integration** — MCP endpoints for ingredients, prices, recipes, suggestions.
- **Alexa backend** — Alexa skill with Node backend connected to recetario-mcp.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Prisma` · `Tailwind CSS` · `NextAuth` · `Google Maps API`
