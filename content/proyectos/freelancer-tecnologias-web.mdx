Personal presentation site as a freelance designer with hero, services (Web Design, Web Apps, E-Commerce), and contact form. Includes a full frontend store (FrontEndStore) with product catalog, detail page, and About section. Responsive layout, multiple interconnected HTML pages. No build step.

> Personal site and static storefront without frameworks

### Destacados

- **Freelancer home** — Hero, services, contact form, and link to store.
- **FrontEnd Store** — Product catalog, product detail, and About page.
- **Responsive design** — Works across devices with Normalize.css for cross-browser consistency.

### Stack

`HTML5` · `CSS3` · `JavaScript` · `Normalize.css`
