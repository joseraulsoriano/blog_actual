React app to simulate scheduling algorithms: priority, burst time, and PID order. Configure 1–8 channels (CPUs), animated timeline, export to CSV/PDF.

> Multi-process scheduling visualization

### Destacados

- **Scheduling algorithms** — Priority (high first), burst (short jobs), ascending PID.
- **Animated timeline** — Per-channel view with time progression and priority colors.
- **Export** — Results to CSV, PDF, and clipboard.

### Stack

`React` · `CSS3` · `jsPDF` · `file-saver`
