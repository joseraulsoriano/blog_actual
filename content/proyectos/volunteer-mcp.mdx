FastAPI MCP with tools for MX volunteering (Worldpackers, Brave, gob.mx domains), education (universities, careers, costs, soft skills), and jobs. URL deduplication, Prometheus metrics, optional Redis, SQLite/Postgres.

> Search and ranking for volunteering, education data, and jobs

### Destacados

- **MX volunteering** — Trusted MX sources, deduplication, apply_link when available.
- **Education** — Rich dataset: universities, careers, tuition, soft skills.
- **CLI and persistence** — education_enricher, education_details scripts; Redis, SQLite/Postgres.

### Stack

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `Brave Search` · `Redis` · `SQLite` · `Postgres` · `Prometheus`
