Full-stack system for credit card analysis: PDF upload, PyPDF2 extraction, bank-specific parsers (Banamex, Amex, MercadoPago, Invex), auto classification, metrics, alerts, and dashboard. Clean architecture (domain, application, infrastructure).

> Statement analysis with local PDF processing

### Destacados

- **Local PDF processing** — LectorDocumentos with PyPDF2: 100% local extraction, no external services.
- **Bank parsers** — Banamex, Amex, MercadoPago, Invex: structured extraction from text.
- **Classification and metrics** — Auto category tagging. Alerts for high APR, credit limit usage.

### Stack

`FastAPI` · `Python` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Tailwind` · `PyPDF2` · `SQLAlchemy` · `Recharts` · `Docker`
